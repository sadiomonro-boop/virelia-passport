An open passport spread for the Republic of Virelia, a fictional fog-bound lighthouse archipelago: the data page of lighthouse keeper Elena Ruth Marlowe with a drawn portrait and a correct MRZ, and a travel-history page stamped by the neighbouring states of Ostara, Kelvar and Dunmar, with a daylight-only condition of entry that reveals what kind of place Virelia is.
